Official Policy

Privacy Policy for CodeRadar

Last updated: November 1, 2026

CodeRadar is an open-source browser extension developed for competitive coders, students, and software engineers. We value your trust and are strictly committed to user privacy.

1. Information Collection and Usage

CodeRadar does NOT collect, transmit, sell, or monetize any personal data.

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not track or collect names, email addresses, browsing history, keystrokes, or location data.
  • No User Accounts: The extension operates anonymously and requires no registration or login.
  • No Analytics / Trackers: There are no third-party tracking scripts, telemetry pixels, or advertisement SDKs bundled.

2. Local Storage and Data Persistence

CodeRadar utilizes Chrome's local storage API (chrome.storage.local) exclusively on your machine to save:

  • User-selected platform preferences (e.g. Codeforces, LeetCode, AtCoder).
  • Contest reminder intervals (e.g. 15 minutes before contest).
  • Bookmarked contests ("Starred" list).
  • Cached public contest schedules to provide instant 0ms offline display.

This data stays on your machine and is never transmitted to external servers.

3. Network Communications

CodeRadar makes direct HTTPS requests only to public contest schedule APIs:

  • https://clist.by - Public contest schedule aggregator.
  • https://codeforces.com - Official Codeforces public contest API.

4. Permissions Justification

  • storage: Keeps your settings and bookmarks on your device.
  • alarms: Schedules background timers to trigger contest reminders on time.
  • notifications: Generates desktop alerts when an opted-in contest is starting soon.
  • Host permissions: Allows fetching public contest schedules.
